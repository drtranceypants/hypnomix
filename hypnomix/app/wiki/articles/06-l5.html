Every decision records who made it and when. A reviewer can leave a note at a bar or a time on any finding. Send them the review file, and when it comes back, merge it: where you both decided the same finding differently, it's shown to you to settle. Your own decision is never overwritten without you seeing it.

$ hypnomix review "hypnomix-out/scan/Song" --export "Song review.json"     # send this
$ hypnomix review "hypnomix-out/scan/Song" --as Reviewer --serve              # they review as "Reviewer"
$ hypnomix review "hypnomix-out/scan/Song" --merge "Song review (Reviewer).json"
$ hypnomix review "hypnomix-out/scan/Song" --settle smooth-4=mine      # settle a conflict
