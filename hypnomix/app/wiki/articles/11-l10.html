Press Record and review before export on HypnomixMain. Live plays, and the device records Main exactly as you hear it (a real-time bounce) while every probe listens. When Live stops, the app reviews it: loudness and true peak against your song card, the live findings, release checks, how it translates to phones, earbuds, cars and streaming codecs, and the mastering moves it would make, as proposals. Fix what you agree with, then export from Live as usual.

The device can't press Export for you, because Live doesn't let any device start an export. The recording it makes is the same audio a real-time export gives, and you can use it as the bounce for every other tool.
