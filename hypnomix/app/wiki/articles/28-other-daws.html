hypnomix can't open Logic, Pro Tools or other DAWs' projects yet, but it can work on their audio. Every check that listens to the recordings works the same: clicks, plosives, hiss, timing, vocal pitch, harmony balance, loudness, release checks.

  1. Export the tracks. Every track as its own file, all from the same start point and the same length, with effects and processing off (raw recordings are what hypnomix fixes). Export a bounce of the mix too if you want the mix and release checks.
  2. Scan the folder. hypnomix scan "Song exports/" --mix "Song mix.wav" changes nothing and writes one review list.
  3. Review. In the app, or hypnomix review <scan folder> --serve: play A and B for each finding, approve or skip.
  4. Apply. Approved audio fixes come back as new files next to the originals; import them in place of the old tracks. Anything that has to be done in your DAW (a fade, a fader move, a mute) is listed as a step to do by hand.

Reading Pro Tools and Logic sessions directly is on the roadmap. Any way of driving hypnomix works here too: choose your setup.
