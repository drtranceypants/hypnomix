HypnoMix has no reader for other DAWs' sessions yet (Live, REAPER and HypnoMix DAW sessions it reads directly), but it can work on their audio. Every check that listens to the recordings works the same: clicks, plosives, hiss, timing, vocal pitch, harmony balance, loudness, release checks.

  1. Export the tracks. Every track as its own file, all from the same start point and the same length, with effects and processing off (raw recordings are what HypnoMix fixes). Export a bounce of the mix too if you want the mix and release checks.
  2. Scan the folder. hypnomix scan "Song exports/" --mix "Song mix.wav" changes nothing and writes one review list.
  3. Review. In the app, or hypnomix review <scan folder> --serve: play A and B for each finding, approve or skip.
  4. Apply. Approved audio fixes come back as new files next to the originals; import them in place of the old tracks. Anything that has to be done in your DAW (a fade, a fader move, a mute) is listed as a step to do by hand.
  5. Hand it back. hypnomix handoff <scan folder> turns that list into a checklist in your DAW's own words, in song order, with what to listen for, plus markers.mid and markers.csv: import one as markers so each spot is marked on your timeline.

Reading the sessions of other commercial DAWs (a session-info text export where there is one, read-only and fragile where the project is a closed bundle) is on the roadmap, for later. Any way of driving HypnoMix works here too: choose your setup.
