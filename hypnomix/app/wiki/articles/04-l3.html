1. Songs: choose a Live set, a REAPER project, an Ardour session, or a folder of recordings from any DAW (or paste its path). A bounce of the mix and the key are optional and add loudness and tuning checks. Press Start the first pass. It only reads. A song you scanned before goes back to its own folder, and Re-scan on its card runs the scan again on the song as it is now (a Live set keeps your decisions).
  2. Activity: every check runs at once (names, timing, clips, vocals, effects, the mix), then the before/after clips are made. You can watch or walk away.
  3. Review: one card per finding, most important first. Each has the problem in plain words, the proposed fix, and A and B players at matched loudness. Choose Approve, Skip or Change (with a note).
  4. Apply: says first, in plain words, what it will do: how many approved fixes, which on-by-default steps will also run (putting tracks in order, removing switched-off tracks after saving a full copy, joining one instrument onto one track: each marked On by default on its card, with Turn off beside it), which approved items are by hand and will not be applied, and the exact file names it will create. Then it writes only that into one new file (.als, .rpp or .ardour, ending as your project does) next to your original, measures again, and shows what was applied, what was not and why, where to listen, and buttons to Undo (a Live set), reopen the review or Make the handoff list.
