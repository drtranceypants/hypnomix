1. Songs: choose a Live set, or a folder of recordings from any DAW. A bounce of the mix and the key are optional and add loudness and tuning checks. Press Start the first pass. It only reads.
  2. Activity: every check runs at once (names, timing, clips, vocals, effects, the mix), then the before/after clips are made. You can watch or walk away.
  3. Review: one card per finding, most important first. Each has the problem in plain words, the proposed fix, and A and B players at matched loudness. Choose Approve, Skip or Change (with a note).
  4. Apply: writes only what you approved into one new set next to your original, then measures again to show what changed and what is still there.
