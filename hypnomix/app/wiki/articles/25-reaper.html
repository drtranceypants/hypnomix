hypnomix works with REAPER projects the same way it works with Live sets: it reads your project, lists what it finds, and writes what you approve into a new project next to the original. REAPER can stay open the whole time. Nothing is installed into REAPER, and none of its settings are changed.

Everything here was checked against REAPER 7.86 itself: hypnomix built test projects inside REAPER, had REAPER render them, and compared the result sample by sample with its own.

What you need

  • REAPER (the free evaluation works for everything here).
  • hypnomix installed (hypnomix doctor checks it).
  • Your project saved as an .rpp with its audio files where REAPER put them. hypnomix finds files the way REAPER does: next to the project, in its recording folder, or anywhere below the project folder.

Only REAPER's own plug-ins (ReaEQ, ReaComp, ReaLimit, the JS effects) are used when hypnomix builds anything. Your own plug-ins are read and listed, never needed.

1 · Look inside a project

$ hypnomix project "Song.rpp"

Prints every track (folders indented), its fader, pan, mute and polarity, its plug-ins (switched-off ones marked), and its automation. Add --items for every item: position, length, which take plays, its file, volume and fades.

$ hypnomix project "Song.rpp" --render

Renders every track you can hear to its own WAV from the start of the song, without opening REAPER: item and take volume, fades and crossfades, loops, sections, playrate and stretch markers are included; faders, plug-ins and automation are not (these are the raw parts, for checking and for stems).

2 · Scan the song

$ hypnomix scan "Song.rpp" --mix "Song.wav"

The same first pass as for a Live set: every check runs and writes one list, REVIEW.md, most serious first. Nothing is changed. For a REAPER project it checks:

groupwhat it looks for
namestracks still called "Track 3" or "Audio 2": named by how they sound; the tracks put in working order (drums, bass, keys and guitars, vocals, other parts; low to high)
filesitems whose audio file is missing
clipsclicks at item edges, level jumps where comp takes meet, short spikes (a pop, a bump), and item edges or fades that cut off the first sound of a word
timinginstrument hits out of time with the band: the bass checked against the drums first, melodic parts only gently; each player's own push or lay-back is kept
vocalsthe pitch of every vocal track, doubles that drift from the lead, how the harmonies sit together
effectsplug-ins that are switched off or offline, two limiters in a row
recordingsevery file the project plays: clipping, levels, hiss, DC offset, harsh "s" sounds, breaths
noiseitems turned up so far that their hiss comes up with them
intentthings that look deliberate (automation, hard pans, muted tracks, big item volumes, flipped polarity): listed as questions, never changed
mixwith --mix: loudness, true peak, release checks, how it translates
stemswith --deep: which parts fight over the same frequencies, and mic phase

Give --ref (the mix or an instrumental) for tuning: without it the key and tuning are read from a rough mix of the project's own tracks.

3 · Decide, item by item

$ hypnomix review "<scan folder>" --serve        # a page: listen, then approve / skip / change
$ hypnomix fix "<scan folder>" --decide clips-3=approve,name-2=skip
$ hypnomix fix "<scan folder>" --decide name-4=approve --note "Acoustic Guitar"

A note changes how a fix is made. For a track name, the note is the name. For a level jump at a comp cut, amount=0.3 takes less of the jump away and method=item sets the item's volume instead of riding the track.

4 · Apply what you approved

$ hypnomix fix "<scan folder>" --approved --out "Song (fixed).rpp"

Everything you approved goes into one new project next to the original:

findingwhat is written
click at an item edgea 5 ms fade on that item (an existing fade is never made shorter)
level jump at a comp cutshort fades either side of the cut, and a volume ride that eases the jump in over 300 ms
spikea quick dip in the track's volume over the burst (at most 3 dB on a low "p" or "b" thump)
a word's first sound cut offthe item edge opened up to just before the consonant, the neighbouring item trimmed to match; the audio stays exactly where it was
a fade ducking a wordthat fade made 10 ms long
track namethe track renamed
track orderthe tracks reordered; folders keep their tracks, and every send still comes from the same track
vocal stackwhere the parts swap tracks, each item moved to the track for its pitch (lowest part on the lowest track), its volume changed by the fader difference so it plays at the same level; items on lane tracks, under automation, or that would overlap another item are listed for you instead
out-of-time hitstretch markers on the item: one on the hit, moved part of the way to where the band (or the drums) plays it, one on the hit either side holding it, so only that short stretch is stretched; drag or delete the markers in REAPER to change or undo one
vocal tuningeach take file pitch-corrected into a new file in Tuned/, added to its items as a new take and made active

Your comp is never replaced by a rendered copy. A tuned vocal is a second take on each of your own items, so switching takes in REAPER gives you the original back. Volume rides go on the track's volume envelope and multiply with what is already there: a ride you drew yourself is kept, and the fader is left alone.

Before anything changes, every approved finding is found again in the project by its item; if the project changed since the scan, it is skipped and said so. Afterwards the new file is read back and checked: the same tracks and folders, the same items in the same places, sends from the same tracks. A file that fails is renamed "(BROKEN, do not open)" and the original stays yours. Then the clip checks run again on the new project, and the log says which approved problems are gone and which are only smaller.

Open the new project in REAPER (File → Open, or drag it in) and A/B the changed spots against the original.

5 · Master in REAPER, with REAPER's own plug-ins

$ hypnomix reaper start
$ hypnomix chain master "Song mix.wav" --reaper --verify

reaper start runs a small hypnomix script inside the REAPER that is open, so hypnomix can add plug-ins and set them. It installs nothing; it stops when REAPER quits, or with hypnomix reaper stop. Every change it makes is one undo step named "hypnomix: ...", so Edit → Undo takes it back.

chain master analyses the bounce of your mix, works out a mastering chain the same way as for Live, and builds it on REAPER's master from stock plug-ins only:

stepREAPER plug-inset to
clean the sub, toneReaEQ (a second one when more than five bands are needed)each band's type, frequency, gain and width
glueReaCompthreshold, 2:1, attack, release, RMS detection, soft knee
mono low endJS: Mid/Side Encoder → ReaEQ → ReaEQ → JS: Mid/Side Decodereverything below the frequency in mono, everything above untouched
warmth (if asked for)JS: Saturationa gentle amount
width (if asked for)JS: Stereo Field Manipulatorwidth %
loudnessReaLimitceiling, and the threshold that gives the gain

Values are set by what the plug-in shows, so a 30 ms attack reads 30.0 in REAPER. Master plug-ins that are already there are never removed: --existing bypass switches them off, --existing keep builds after them.

With --verify, REAPER renders the loudest bars of the song, hypnomix measures them, and ReaLimit is trimmed until they match the target. Then the whole song is rendered once more for true peak: ReaLimit limits the samples, and the signal between samples can still go over (on one finished song it reached -0.43 dBTP with a -1 dB ceiling). If it does, the ceiling is pulled down until the whole song is under. No routing through another app is needed: REAPER writes the file itself.

chain strips recipe.json --reaper builds a channel strip on every track from an automix recipe (ReaEQ, ReaComp, fader and pan). Sends to a reverb are listed for you to add.

6 · Talk to the REAPER that is open

$ hypnomix reaper status        # REAPER version, open project, tempo, tracks
$ hypnomix reaper tracks        # each track with its plug-ins, fader and pan
$ hypnomix reaper fx Rea        # installed plug-ins whose name has "Rea" in it
$ hypnomix reaper params master 0

params lists every parameter of one plug-in as the plug-in shows it (the track by name, number or master; the plug-in by name or number).

Not yet in REAPER

  • Sidechain and ducking checks on plug-ins, and the probe devices (Live only).
  • Where a stretch marker squeezes or stretches a segment, hypnomix's own render reads it at a slightly different pitch than REAPER plays it (REAPER keeps the pitch). The timing is exact.
