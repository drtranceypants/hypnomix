With the optional plugins extra, hypnomix loads your VST3 and AU plug-ins outside Live and restores each one to the settings saved in your set. That way it can render a track through its actual chain, and the before/after clips for device changes include your plug-ins too. Each plug-in is tried in a separate process first, so one that crashes is reported and skipped and the render carries on.

$ hypnomix plugrender "Song.als" --list             # each chain, and what can be rendered
$ hypnomix plugrender "Song.als" --check            # load and restore every plug-in, no audio
$ hypnomix plugrender "Song.als" --tracks "Lead Vox" "Bass"
$ hypnomix ab "hypnomix-out/scan/Song" --plugins    # before/after for device fixes, through the chain
Live's own Utility, Compressor, Glue Compressor, Limiter, Gate and EQ Eight are modelled (approximations of the devices, not Live itself). Other Live devices, racks and Max for Live devices are skipped and named in the report. Without --plugins, ab still previews a change to one of those six devices through the models alone, and says so on the pair; with it, your real plug-ins are in the chain too. Some Audio Units refuse to load outside a DAW; when a plug-in also comes as a VST3, that version is used instead.
