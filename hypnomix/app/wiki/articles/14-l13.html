vocalign lines doubles and harmonies up with the lead in time. If you ask, it also matches pitch and formant, checking each note against the chord under it. Presets cover the usual jobs, and you can protect any bars you want left exactly as sung. Inside Live, alignset writes the timing as warp markers on your own clips in a new set, and the Hypnomix Align device does it from inside Live. In REAPER, alignset on a .rpp writes stretch markers (or new takes) on your own items in a new project, and the HypnoMix Align panel does it from inside REAPER.

$ hypnomix vocalign --list-presets
$ hypnomix vocalign "Lead.wav" "Double.wav" "Harmony hi.wav" --preset natural-double --protect 33-36
$ hypnomix vocalign "Lead.wav" "Harmony hi.wav" --pitch --chords "Song instrumental.wav"
$ hypnomix alignset "Song.als" --guide "Lead Vox" --parts "Double" "Harmony Hi" --to-average --apply   # warp markers on your own clips, in a new set
$ hypnomix aligndevice                  # the Align device for Live
$ hypnomix reaper align                 # the Align panel in REAPER (a new project; yours untouched)
