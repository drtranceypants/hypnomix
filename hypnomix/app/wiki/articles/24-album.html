Each song can pass qc and still sound wrong next to the one before it: one a few dB quieter, one duller, one that stops dead into the next. album compares the songs with each other. It writes ALBUM.md and changes nothing.

$ hypnomix album "Song A.wav" "Song B.wav" "Song C.wav" "Song D.wav"
$ hypnomix album "Masters/" --order "Song D,Song A,Song C,Song B" --suggest-order
$ hypnomix album "Masters/" --anchor "Song A" --tolerance 1.5 --plan

What it measures, per song

Integrated loudness, the loudest section (the highest short-term loudness, 3 second windows), true peak, the tonal curve in 8 bands against the median of the other songs, stereo width, the silence at the head and tail and how the song ends and starts (cut off, short fade, ring-out, long fade; full strength or soft), key and tempo. --quick skips key and tempo, the slow part.

What it says across the album

  • Level. A quiet ballad is allowed to be quieter, so two readings are taken: the whole song and its loudest section. By default (--reading both) a song counts as apart only when both are more than --tolerance (2 LU) away in the same direction. A song that is lower only as a whole (quiet verses) is shown and left alone. --reading whole or loudest uses one reading. --target -14 compares with a fixed figure instead of the other songs, --anchor "Song A" matches the others to one song, and --keep-quiet "Song C" says a song is meant to be quieter and leaves it alone.
  • Tone. Which songs are brighter, darker, boomier, thinner or boxier than the rest, band by band, and by how many dB (--tone-tolerance, 1.5 dB). For the highs it uses whichever of the presence band (4 to 8 kHz) and the air band (8 kHz and up) is furthest from the others, so a boost that starts at 6 kHz is not averaged away. The sub-bass has to be twice as far off to count. Only shape counts: each curve is lined up on its own 250 Hz to 4 kHz level first.
  • Width. Songs clearly wider or narrower than the rest. Reported only.
  • The gap between each pair, in your order. It is suggested from how the first song's tail decays and how the next one starts. A song that stops abruptly gets room to land. A long fade needs little silence. A song that comes in at full strength gets a little more room before it, and a big drop in level gets a breath. --gap-style tight|normal|roomy scales them, and --gap 1.5 uses one figure. It also names how far apart the keys are and the tempo change, so a hard cut between distant keys is a decision, not an accident.
  • Order options (--suggest-order, three songs or more): one or two orders, by energy arc and by key relation, listed as options with their key changes counted. They are never applied; you decide the running order.

With only three songs the "median of the others" is just their midpoint, so one odd song pulls the rest; the report says so, and --anchor settles which song is the standard. With two songs each is measured against the midpoint of the two, so each move is half the gap and the two meet in the middle instead of swapping places. A song that is only quiet or loud on its own account is offered a move "only if you want it to match the others"; nothing is changed unless you approve it.

Turning proposals into moves: --plan

ALBUM.md lists what could change. Add --plan and each proposal becomes a finding in a scan folder, with the usual review:

  • A gain trim for a song that sits apart, by the smaller of the two gaps, never past --ceiling (-1 dBTP): what is left needs a limiter (master).
  • A gentle tonal tilt: a low shelf (at 160 Hz) and/or a high shelf (at 5 kHz), --tilt-amount 50% of the gap, never more than --max-tilt-db (2 dB), loudness kept.
  • Trimmed or padded silence so the gaps match the suggestion. Only silence is touched; nothing above -60 dBFS is cut.
$ hypnomix album "Masters/" --plan album-scan
$ hypnomix ab album-scan                          # before and after for each move, level-matched
$ hypnomix fix album-scan --decide album-1=approve,album-2=skip
$ hypnomix fix album-scan --approved             # → album-scan/fixed/: new files, your songs untouched

Several approved moves on one song chain, each starting from the file the last one wrote. The file with the highest step, for example Song A (album gain+tilt+ends).wav, is the finished one, and each step before it is kept. The silence moves belong to the order you gave: if you change the order, plan again.

Measured, not heard. The gaps and the order are taste calls: listen to each transition with the next song queued before you decide. See also qc (one file), tonal (a target from your own songs) and master.
