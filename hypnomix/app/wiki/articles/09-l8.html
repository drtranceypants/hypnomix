The probe measures each track ten times a second, after every device and plug-in on it, which is what you actually hear. A full scan used to render each track without its effects. Now one play of the song measures them all, third-party plug-ins included. Press Record in the Live tab, play the song (or just the part you're working on), press Stop, and you get a normal review:

  • frequency fights between tracks while they play together, with the band and who should give way (your priority settings decide)
  • the lead vocal's level against the band, section by section
  • a low end that isn't mono, a mix that's too wide or narrow
  • Main's loudness and peak against your song card
  • tracks running hot into the next plug-in
$ hypnomix probe status                         # who's sending, loudness right now
$ hypnomix probe listen --bars 49-64 --play     # Live plays the chorus; a review comes back
