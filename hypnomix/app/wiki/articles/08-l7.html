1. Drag HypnomixProbe onto the last slot of one track and HypnomixMain onto Main. Save the set.
  2. Run hypnomix probe insert "Song.als". It writes a new set with a probe on every track, return and group (each starting at "auto").
  3. Set the menus where you know better than a guess. They're Live parameters, so they're saved in your set, and the next scan reads them. Leave "auto" anywhere you're happy for hypnomix to work it out by sound.
Modes such as Dorian or Mixolydian are tuned with the notes of their parent major key, so A Dorian is tuned to the notes of G major. Chords you type are kept by the app, matched to the song by its track names.

A mastering chain of Live's own devices

  1. Build chain puts a rack called Hypnomix Master just before HypnomixMain: Tone (EQ Eight), Glue (Glue Compressor), Tame (Multiband Dynamics, off), Warmth (Saturator, off), Width (Utility: bass mono below 120 Hz) and a Limiter. Every gain starts at 0 dB, so it starts neutral. Open the rack and change anything you like.
  2. While it's the rack hypnomix built, it follows the song card: the Ceiling sets the Limiter's ceiling, and the Limiter character (clean, punch, loud) sets its lookahead, its release and the Glue's attack.
  3. Play the loudest part and press Match target. It listens to Main after the chain and turns the Limiter's gain until Main sits on the card's Target LUFS, in a few rounds of about 8 seconds each.
  4. Type a name and press Save to keep the chain as it is now (your edits, or a rack of your own) as a template. Pick it from the menu next time and press Build chain. Plug-ins and Max for Live devices can't be rebuilt by Live, so they're listed as left out.
Live adds each device with your default preset, if you have set one. Build empties the rack first, then sets the values above on top of your default device presets. Anything it doesn't list keeps your preset's value. Pressing Build again asks before it replaces the rack. Tested in Live 12.4: in one test Match target settled at -9.9 LUFS for a -10 target.
