The loop is scan, hear, decide, apply. It is easier to learn on something you cannot damage. demo makes a song of about 40 seconds (96 BPM, A minor) from scratch: nothing is downloaded, nothing is shipped, and the same seed always gives the same audio. It is synthetic and sounds like it; the point is the workflow.

$ hypnomix demo --kind reaper
# → …/Hypnomix Demo Song/Hypnomix Demo Song.rpp, the stems in Audio/, and DEMO.md
$ hypnomix scan "…/Hypnomix Demo Song.rpp" --key "A minor"
$ hypnomix ab "<the scan folder>"
$ hypnomix fix "<the scan folder>" --decide clips-1=approve,rec-2=approve
$ hypnomix fix "<the scan folder>" --approved --out "…/Hypnomix Demo Song (fixed).rpp"

The ids are in the scan's REVIEW.md: those two are the click fade and the de-hiss. Approve what you like and the original project and files stay as they are; the fixes are new files and a new project.

On a REAPER project ab plays repaired recordings and says why the other fixes have no clip, so here the de-hiss has a before and after clip and the click fade does not. For the others, apply one fix into a new project and listen there, or build a project with every fix switched off next to yours with abset. hypnomix handoff then lists what is left for your hands.

The six problems

#what is wrongwherewhat finds it
1a click: the keys' two takes meet with no fade0:14.2, Keysscan (clips), clicks
2a level jump: the lead's second take is 7 dB louder, joined in a held note0:27, Lead Vocalscan (clips)
3hiss, steady, heard in the pausesall of Harmonyscan (recordings), fixed by dehiss
4clipped peaks: the bass recorded too hotbars 7 and 8, Bassscan (recordings), prep declip
5a harmony that drifts sharp, up to 45 centsfrom 0:20, Harmonyscan (vocals), tune
6hits that drift late, 20 ms up to 65 msbars 9 to 14, Drumsscan (timing)

Two kinds

  • --kind folder (the default) writes the stems as WAV files in a new folder, as you would export them from any DAW. The comps are flattened, so the click and the level jump are already in the audio: a scan of the folder finds the hiss and the clipping, and clicks lists the click.
  • --kind reaper also writes a REAPER project that plays them, with the two comps as separate items, so scan sees all six. REAPER does not need to be open to scan it. The project loads and round-trips through hypnomix's own reader, but it has not yet been opened in REAPER.

There is no Live set kind yet: a set written from scratch has not been opened in Live, so it is left out until it has. DEMO.md in the new folder lists each problem with its time and the command that finds it, and a folder that already exists is never reused. The same --seed (7 unless you change it) always gives the same audio. Your real work is never involved: everything is made in a new folder (hypnomix-out/demo/, or the one you give with --out).
