hypnomix reads your REAPER project, lists what it finds, and writes only what you approve into a new project next to the original. Nothing is installed into REAPER and none of its settings change. Every step here was checked against REAPER 7.86 itself: hypnomix built test projects inside REAPER, had REAPER render them, and compared the result sample by sample with its own.

1 · Look inside a project

$ hypnomix project "Song.rpp"            # tracks, folders, fader, pan, plug-ins, automation
$ hypnomix project "Song.rpp" --items    # every item: position, take, file, volume, fades
$ hypnomix project "Song.rpp" --render   # each track to a WAV, without opening REAPER

Renders include item and take volume, fades and crossfades, loops, sections, playrate and stretch markers; not faders, plug-ins or automation (these are the raw parts, for checking and for stems).

2 · Scan, decide, fix

$ hypnomix scan "Song.rpp" --mix "Song mix.wav"                     # read-only → REVIEW.md
$ hypnomix review "hypnomix-out/scan/Song" --serve                  # a page: listen, approve / skip / change
$ hypnomix fix "hypnomix-out/scan/Song" --decide name-4=approve --note "Acoustic Guitar"
$ hypnomix fix "hypnomix-out/scan/Song" --approved --out "Song (fixed).rpp"

The scan checks track names and order, missing files, clicks, level jumps at comp cuts, spikes, item edges that cut off the first sound of a word, vocal pitch and doubles, plug-ins switched off, the source recordings, boosted hiss, and things that look deliberate (asked about, never changed). fix writes everything you approved into one new project:

findingwhat is written
click at an item edgea 5 ms fade (an existing fade is never made shorter)
level jump at a comp cutshort fades either side, and a volume ride that eases the jump in over 300 ms
spike (a pop, a bump)a quick dip in the track's volume over it (at most 3 dB on a "p" or "b" thump)
a word's first sound cut offthe item edge opened to just before the consonant; the audio stays exactly where it was
track names and orderrenamed and reordered; folders keep their tracks, sends still come from the same tracks
vocal tuningeach take tuned into a new file and added to its items as a new take, made active

Your comp is never replaced by a rendered copy: a tuned vocal is a second take on each of your own items, so switching takes in REAPER gives you the original back. Volume rides multiply with what is already on the track's envelope, so a ride you drew yourself is kept and the fader is left alone. Every finding is found again before anything changes, the new file is read back and checked (same tracks, folders, items, sends), and the clip checks run again to say which problems are gone.

3 · Master in REAPER, with REAPER's own plug-ins

$ hypnomix reaper start                                      # a small script inside the open REAPER
$ hypnomix chain master "Song mix.wav" --reaper --verify

reaper start installs nothing and stops when REAPER quits (or hypnomix reaper stop). Every change it makes is one undo step, so Edit → Undo takes it back. The chain is built on REAPER's master from stock plug-ins only:

stepREAPER plug-in
clean the sub, toneReaEQ
glueReaComp
mono low endJS Mid/Side Encoder → ReaEQ → ReaEQ → JS Mid/Side Decoder (only the bass goes mono; the stereo image above is untouched)
warmth, width (if asked for)JS Saturation, JS Stereo Field Manipulator
loudnessReaLimit

With --verify, REAPER renders the loudest bars and ReaLimit is trimmed to your target; then the whole song is rendered for true peak, and the ceiling is pulled down if the signal between samples goes over (REAPER writes the file itself: no extra routing app). Plug-ins already on the master are never removed: --existing bypass switches them off, --existing keep builds after them.

4 · Talk to the REAPER that is open

$ hypnomix reaper status            # version, open project, tempo, tracks
$ hypnomix reaper tracks            # each track with its plug-ins, fader and pan
$ hypnomix reaper fx Rea            # installed plug-ins with "Rea" in the name
$ hypnomix reaper params master 0   # every parameter of one plug-in, as it shows it
