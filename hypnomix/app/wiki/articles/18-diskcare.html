Every scan, A/B comparison and render leaves audio behind, and a few songs can fill tens of gigabytes. The app looks after this itself: there's nothing to install or schedule. The free space shows at the top right; click it to see what would go, clean now, or switch it off.

When it runs

While the app is open it checks once an hour. It cleans when a week has passed since the last clean, or straight away when the disk is nearly full: under 8% of the disk, counted as at most 50 GB and at least 20 GB, so a big disk with 70 GB left is not called full. When the disk is nearly full it also uses shorter ages and shows a banner.

On a Mac it sends a notification only when the news is worth hearing: it freed more than 1 GB, the disk got fuller than at its last notice, or once a day while the disk stays nearly full. It never repeats the same warning every hour.

The app's own scans are always included. If you scan into your project folders (from the command line or an AI agent), add those folders under Also look in and their hypnomix scans and hypnomix-out folders are looked after too.

Because Disk care deletes old audio inside the folders you add, each one must be a folder that exists inside your home folder. It can't be your whole home folder, the top of the disk, Library, or all of Music, Desktop or Documents at once: pick the folder your projects are in (a folder inside those is fine). If you add one that isn't allowed, the app says why and keeps the list as it was. A folder you saved with an earlier version that isn't allowed now (a project folder on an external drive, say) is no longer cleaned, but it is still read, so the audio its projects use stays protected.

What it never touches

  • Any audio file a Live, REAPER or Ardour project on this computer uses. Projects under Music, Documents, Desktop and the folders you added are read to check.
  • Files with "final" or "master" in their name, on the Desktop and in Downloads.
  • Anything changed in the last hour, and anything while a job is running.
  • Your projects, their Samples folders, findings, decisions and reports.

On a Mac, the first time it looks in Desktop, Documents or Downloads macOS asks whether hypnomix may. If you said no, the preview names the folders it couldn't read: allow hypnomix in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Files and Folders.

From a terminal: python -m hypnomix.app.diskcare shows what would go, and --run cleans now.
