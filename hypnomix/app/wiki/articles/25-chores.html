the chorewhat hypnomix doescommands
Hunting clicks, pops and level jumpsFinds clicks at clip edges and loop seams, jumps where one comped clip cuts to the next, plosives and spikes, and eases each with a short fade or dip. It also catches the opposite mistake: a fade or comp edit that cuts into a word.smooth edges clicks
Finding the hit that's lateDetects the song's own grid (straight, triplets or a mix), locks the low end first (bass to the drums), compares every hit with the rest of the band, and nudges only the clear outliers part of the way. Drums go first, then the bass toward the corrected drums, then everything else. Audio gets warp markers you can still drag; MIDI drums and bass are checked too.timing tighten unroll
Tuning a vocal note by noteMeasures the song's real tuning and key (section by section), then moves only each note's centre. Vibrato, scoops and slides stay as sung. Where the key is unsure it asks, and leaves those notes alone until you answer.tune key
Lining up doubles and harmoniesWorks word by word: only the words clearly out of time with the other parts are moved, the rest stay bit-exact. In Live or REAPER it writes onto your own clips or items, in a new set or project.vocalign alignset
Balancing a harmony stackLists each part with its note and level, asks which one leads, and turns down only the parts that overshadow it, by the amount you pick or the spacing you use in your own finished songs. It is judged as heard, after each track's effects, and written as volume automation.voices cliplevel
Chasing hiss, hum, breaths and "s" soundsSpectral de-hiss, hum and rumble removal, breaths turned down (not off), a split-band de-esser that lists every "s" it touched, clipped peaks rebuilt.prep dehiss deess breaths
Finding what's distortingClipped takes, drive set too hot, limiters working too hard, compressors releasing faster than a bass note: each named with its track, bar and a setting to try.distortion
Setting up sidechains and duckingMeasures how deep each sidechain ducks and whether it recovers in time, proposes settings for the style you want, and can make the vocal reverb and delay step back while the singer sings.sidechain duck
Naming, ordering and tidying 40 tracksNames every track by how it sounds, puts them in working order (drums, bass, keys and guitars, vocals) and puts each harmony on the track for its pitch, so one fader is one voice. Switched-off old takes are retired after a full archive copy of the set is saved.name order tidy
Checking every compressor and gatePlays each track through its own chain and judges every stock device on the signal it really gets: a compressor that barely works, squashes, or lets go too fast for a bass note; a gate that chops words. It suggests threshold, attack and release for that part and tempo.devices
Mapping a take played without a clickFollows the beat through the take, writes a click check for your ears, then either warps the take onto the grid or makes Live's tempo follow the player. It also picks the best loops and lines them up to try.tempomap loops
Making a looped song feel aliveSmall tempo pushes into choruses, filter sweeps and delay throws in the style of your own automation, and unused takes or loops from the original project offered on new, switched-off tracks.breathe motion variations borrow
Working out which parts clashWhich two parts fight over which frequencies, how often, and the exact cut that fixes it. Mic pairs checked for phase and polarity.masking align stems
Getting loud without wrecking itA mastering chain set by measurement, test-encoded to AAC and MP3 so it won't clip after upload, built offline or inside Live from stock devices, or as an editable rack on HypnomixMain that you save as your own template.master chain probe
The pre-release checklistPhone, earbuds, car and mono checks; how much each streaming service will turn it down; silence, true peak, phase and format. Section by section: "the second chorus is 2 LU quieter than the first".translate qc sections match
Packaging the releaseOne folder to upload: your master as 24-bit WAV (untouched), a dithered 16-bit WAV, MP3 320 and AAC 256 with tags and cover, and a report that measures every file, including the true peak after the lossy ones are decoded. --check first.release
Instrumental, a cappella, TV mix, radio editFrom your own exported stems, no separation. Checks the stems really add up to your mix, matches each version to its loudness with gain only, crossfades the cuts of a radio edit (to bar lines with --bpm) and runs the release checks on every file.versions
Is the new bounce better than the last one?Lines the two up and says in plain words what changed: loudness, peaks, dynamics, tone and stereo width by band, each section, with level-matched A/B clips of the three spots that differ most.compare
Making an EP or album sit togetherThe songs side by side in plain words: which sit apart in level (judged on the loudest section as well as the whole, so a quiet ballad may be quieter), which are brighter, darker or boomier and by how much, the gap each pair could have, and order options. Any trim, gentle tilt or silence change is a finding you approve, applied into new files.album
Checking the files before you startMixed sample rates or bit depths and which file is the odd one out, stems of different length or that start apart, duplicates, fake stereo, silent or clipped files, lossy sources, missing files, names that will not travel. Report first; prep repairs what it can.preflight
Learning the loop on nothing of your ownA 40-second synthetic song with six labelled problems (a click, a level jump, hiss, clipped peaks, a drifting harmony, late hits), as stems or a REAPER project: scan it, hear each fix, apply two.demo

What stays yours: the playing, the sounds, the effects, the arrangement and every taste call. hypnomix asks before anything that changes how the song feels, shows what is deliberate (your automation, your long reverb tail) on every review so nobody "fixes" it, and never overwrites a file.
