Road-testing the newest tools. The tempo, variety and motion tools pass their tests and structure checks; the next step is many more real songs through them in Live.

New colours. hypnomix colour is built for Live sets (how it works): it finds the parts that sound the same every time round and proposes a chain of Live's own devices on a switched-off copy, each with a before-and-after clip. Next: REAPER and Ardour projects, and an instrument doubling a line.

More of the review in one place. A first balance for a folder of stems (balance), the by-hand list in your DAW's words (handoff), masking cuts written as stock EQ bands, and previews for timing nudges and device changes are all built; next comes hearing them on many more real songs.

Beyond Live. REAPER already goes the whole way: scan a project, write what you approve into a new .rpp, and master in the open REAPER with its own plug-ins. It has its own versions of the Live devices too: Hypnomix Probe and Main (meters, the song card, Record and review, a mastering chain with Build, Match target and Save), the A/B review panel, and the Hypnomix Align panel that lines up a vocal stack in a new project (hypnomix in REAPER). Ardour sessions can be read and scanned, and fix writes approved fades, gain and dips into a new .ardour (written and re-read by hypnomix; not yet opened in Ardour); next, hands-on control of the open session with Ardour's own plug-ins. Pro Tools and Logic Pro follow. Today, scan, prep, automix, master and the vocal tools already work on a plain folder of recordings or stems from any DAW.
