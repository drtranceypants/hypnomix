The web app. One command opens it in your browser; it runs on your computer only. Choose a Live set, or a folder of recordings from any DAW, and press one button: it runs every check and builds the before-and-after clips. The review shows each finding with play buttons for A and B and three choices: Approve, Skip, Change. Apply writes only what you approved into a new set next to your original, then shows exactly what changed. Your preferences and every other hypnomix tool are a click away, each with a form.

The Live device. A Max for Live device you drop on any track; audio passes through it untouched. It shows the finding you are on and steps through them, and Live jumps to each one: the track is selected, the playhead moves to the spot, the loop brace goes around it and the clip opens. Approve or skip right there. "Show in Live" buttons in the app do the same jump. Press Float (top right of the device) and the same panel opens in a small floating window: a jump selects the finding's track, so Live shows that track's clip where the device was, and the floating panel stays in view. The device never edits the set it sits in: applying still happens in the app, into a new set.

The Teacher tab. A guided course in your own DAW: a tour of hypnomix and its devices if you already engineer, or a 101 crash course (recording to a click, each effect, mastering) if you're new to audio. It can do the hands-on steps in Live and REAPER for you, asking first each time. Teacher mode.

The Wiki. Every lesson, how-to guide, command and FAQ answer on this site is also in the app's Wiki tab, with search, and it works offline. When the website has newer articles, the app shows those: it only downloads the articles, and you can switch that off in the tab.

The Assistant and the Listener (optional). Rather talk than click? The Assistant tab is a chat with the AI of your choice that runs hypnomix for you, and a Listener, a Google model with its own key, can hear a bounce and give notes by ear. Both use your own keys, and each listen waits for your click. How to set them up.

Both run on your computer only. Nothing about your songs is sent anywhere unless you set up a Listener and click Send it, and the app refuses requests from other sites. The device needs Max for Live (Live Suite, or the add-on). It has been loaded and used in Live 12 Suite on a real song; it is new, so tell us what breaks.

New: measure every track while Live plays, tell hypnomix what each track is and what the song is, A/B every fix inside Live with a blind test, build an editable mastering chain of Live’s own devices that matches your loudness target, and record a review before export. All of it has been tested in Live 12. See the lessons in this wiki for step-by-step pictures and videos.

The Mac and Windows app

The easiest way to get it: the installer for Mac or Windows from the download page. On a Mac it's Hypnomix.app, a window of its own; on Windows the Start menu opens it in your browser. The first time it opens, a guided setup asks how you'd like to work (on your own, with an AI key, with a free model on your computer, or with Claude Code), which DAW you use and whether you'd like teacher mode, and shows the costs and the pros and cons of each: Setting Up, and Choosing an AI.

Installed from the source? You can build the same Mac app yourself in about a minute, made for (and trusted by) your own Mac:

1. Install Apple's command line tools once, if you haven't: xcode-select --install.
2. In the hypnomix folder, run macapp/build.sh.
3. Open macapp/dist/Hypnomix.app, or drag it into Applications.

If you move or reinstall the hypnomix folder, run the build again. The web app and the Mac app are the same thing inside; use whichever you like.

$ xcode-select --install     # once
$ cd hypnomix && macapp/build.sh
$ open macapp/dist/Hypnomix.app
