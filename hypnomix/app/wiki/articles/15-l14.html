$ hypnomix scan "Song.als" --quick                 # structure, edges, levels: seconds, no rendering
$ hypnomix scan "Song.als" --since "hypnomix-out/scan/Song"   # only the tracks you changed
$ hypnomix watch "Song.als"                        # every time you save in Live, a quick check of what changed

Rendered clips are cached by their source file and settings, so a second scan of the same song re-renders nothing it has already rendered. The library scan, the names and the pitch analysis are cached the same way.
