First pass

scan a song, hear each fix, approve, apply

appnew
The whole first pass in a window: choose a song, hear each fix, approve, apply. No terminal and no AI needed.
devicenew
A Max for Live device: step through the findings inside Live, and Live jumps to each one.
Teacher modenew
The app's Teacher tab teaches audio production in your own DAW (Live, REAPER or HypnoMix Studio): the complete course from what sound is to releasing a song (13 chapters, 138 to 146 lessons depending on your DAW, with audio examples, quizzes and a glossary), a 101 crash course, or a tour of HypnoMix. Free, and no AI needed.
prefs
Asked once: your loudness targets, tracks to protect, what is deliberate, and how you like to review. It checks every answer (a loudness target of abc is refused with the numbers that would work) and remembers what you usually approve and skip.
demonew
A 40-second synthetic song with six labelled problems (a click, a level jump, hiss, clipped peaks, a drifting harmony, late hits), as stems or a REAPER project, to learn the whole loop on nothing of your own.
preflightnew
Checks the files before any work: mixed sample rates or bit depths, stems of different length or that start apart, duplicates, fake stereo, silent or clipped files, lossy sources, missing files, names other systems refuse. Report first; prep repairs what it can.
scan
Runs every check on a Live set, a REAPER project, a HypnoMix Studio session, or a folder of recordings or stems from any DAW. Changes nothing. Writes REVIEW.md, most severe first, with each kind of fix explained once and every item holding only what is its own, and lists any check that could not run. On a REAPER project it also judges each ReaComp (devices) and checks the project file itself (structure: it reads back exactly, folders close, sends come from real tracks, faders at the top or nearly silent; listed only). A re-scan keeps your decisions on their own findings and never reuses another song's folder.
projectnew
Looks inside a REAPER project or a HypnoMix Studio session without opening the DAW: tracks, buses, items or regions, takes, plug-ins and automation. --render renders each track to a full-length file.
reapernew
Talks to the REAPER that is open: starts the bridge, lists tracks, installed plug-ins and their parameters. reaper snapshot saves every fader, pan, mute, solo, send and plug-in setting, puts one back as a single undo step, and says what changed between two in plain words; reaper loop --bars 33-40 renders and measures that stretch against the previous take, with a one-change A/B heard at matched loudness; reaper diagnose solos each folder in the loudest bars and says who owns the sub, the bass and the vocal band; each run keeps its files in a new numbered folder (run-001, run-002 …), so an earlier run is never replaced. HypnoMix Probe and HypnoMix Main (REAPER effects) and the Review and Align panels draw their own modern windows with live meters, and each has a built-in step-by-step guide behind its ?. The snapshot, loop and diagnose tools are new: run on a two-track test project in REAPER 7.86 (2026-11-26), not on a real song and not judged by ear; diagnose has not been tried on a project with folders. The windows and their guides were opened and captured by screenshot, not clicked through by hand, and nothing was tried on Windows.
ab
Before-and-after clips for each proposed fix, at matched loudness, so you hear it before you approve it: clip fixes, tuning, timing nudges, masking cuts, and changes to six of Live's stock devices (Compressor, Glue Compressor, Gate, EQ Eight, Utility, Limiter), the last labelled as a model of the device, not Live itself.
review
The whole review as one page: play A, play B, click approve, skip or change. Approve part of a move with an amount, decide the ones like this together, read why each technique works, leave notes, and merge a collaborator's review.
fix
Applies only what you approved into one new set, then measures it again to show which problems are gone. Refuses a set that changed since the scan, never overwrites a file, asks again before a protected track. Before it writes it lists every file it is about to create and the clean-ups that are on by default (--no-defaults leaves them out); afterwards it lists what it did not apply and what to do next. fix DIR --list --undecided --top 15 pages through a long scan and --show ID prints one finding in full.
check · diff
Every set HypnoMix writes gets a structure check before you open it, and a plain list of what changed. Tracks are matched by id, so a new order or new names show as expected changes, not errors.
taste
Reads your own finished songs: fader levels against the lead, panning, reverb and delay amounts, device chains, what you automate. What you always do becomes a standard; what varies becomes a question. --library studies every set on your computer at once (in seconds after the first run) and adds where you could improve, with the evidence.
profile
Your sound as measured ranges, from songs you love.
handoffnew
Turns the by-hand list into a checklist in your DAW's own words, in song order, with what to listen for and a marker at each spot: locators in a new Live set, markers in a new REAPER project, or a MIDI marker file and CSV for any other DAW.
historynew
Every version fix wrote for a song: list them, see what changed between two, undo to any version or rebuild without one fix. Undo always writes a new set.
watchnew
Keeps an eye on a set while you work: every time you save, a quick check of only what changed. Read-only.
importnew
Brings a project from another DAW across as a NEW HypnoMix Studio session (import PROJECT OUTDIR, or Import in the DAW view's engine panel): a .als, .logicx, .dawproject or .rpp project, and with the HypnoMix Studio add-on (Apple-silicon Macs only so far) a .ptx session or an .aaf export. Tracks and their regions with the audio copied, and what each file type holds of faders, pans, mutes and tempo; plug-ins listed by name where the file type has them; IMPORT-REPORT.md lists what was left out. The project is only read. What each file type carries.
als2ardournew
Brings a Live set across as a NEW HypnoMix Studio session folder (als2ardour SET.als OUTDIR): tracks, clips, fades, gain, automation and tempo, with the audio copied (written and re-read by HypnoMix; not yet opened in a released DAW). The Live project is only read, and EQ Eight, Glue Compressor and Saturator come across as ACE EQ, ACE Compressor and Hypnomix Saturator with their settings; IMPORT-REPORT.md lists what they could not take and everything that was not carried (other devices, plug-ins, warp maps, Session clips). HypnoMix Studio.
storenew
Free and open-source plug-ins, synths, instruments, drum kits and sample packs, from their publishers' own downloads: search, info, install, uninstall, updates. Mac only for now. Nothing downloaded is run, and a download that cannot be checked against a SHA-256 is refused unless you confirm it with --unverified. The app's Store tab does the same. The store.
enginenew
HypnoMix Studio, the add-on (HypnoMix's own DAW; engine 0.1.0, Apple-silicon Macs only, not available for Intel Macs or Windows yet; what it is built on): engine says what is added, engine where the folder, and engine install / engine remove report what they would do and change nothing until you add --yes. A fetch is checked against a SHA-256 before anything is unpacked; the engine is a separate program under the GNU General Public License (version 2 or later), HypnoMix itself is MIT.

Clean-up in the set

the first tidy of a session, without opening Live

name
Names every track and group by how it sounds: drums, bass, guitar, strings, lead vocal, harmonies, keys, pads.
ordernew
Tracks in working order, and each part of a vocal stack on the track for its pitch. Never changes the sound. On a HypnoMix Studio session it proposes groups, written into the open engine only for the ids you pass with --engine --ids.
tidynew
Retires switched-off tracks (after an archive copy with every take) and puts one instrument's clips on one track. Clips may overlap only where the overlap is silence, which is cut away: no sound is ever overwritten. On a HypnoMix Studio session it proposes a group per instrument, written into the open engine only with --engine --ids.
devicesnew
Checks every stock compressor, gate, limiter and EQ low cut by what it does to its track, and suggests settings. Applying or previewing one suggestion judges only the track it is on (the numbers stay the scan's), and the checks around a fix look at only the tracks it changes. In a REAPER project each ReaComp is judged on its own track's signal and fix writes the suggested threshold, ratio, attack and release into a new project (read back exactly on a two-track test project in REAPER 7.86 (2026-11-26), not on a real song and not judged by ear); ReaGate and ReaLimit are listed, not judged, and so is a ReaComp with Wet at 0 or with dry and wet mixed (parallel compression), with no fix. Plug-ins on the master track are not read. In a HypnoMix Studio session each ACE Compressor is judged the same way (its Range, Auto Release and Mix included), with the settings listed to set by hand; ACE EQ's High Pass band counts as a low cut, and a hard-driven Hypnomix Saturator gets a lower Drive suggested. In a Live set a Compressor or Glue Compressor with Dry/Wet under 97 % is parallel compression: listed with what you hear, not judged.
smooth
Clicks at clip edges and loop seams, level jumps at cuts, and real pops: eased with short fades and dips. A consonant that opens a sung phrase is left alone; plosives get at most 3 dB.
timing
Detects the song's grid (straight, triplets or mixed), finds hits out of time with the band and nudges only those, part of the way. In passes: drums, then bass toward the drums, then the rest. Audio and MIDI.
distortion
Audible distortion in takes and device chains (clipped takes, hot drive, fast bass compression), with a fix for each.
sidechain
Measures every sidechain's ducking (depth, recovery, timing) and suggests settings for clarity, pump or vocal ducking. Also on a REAPER project or a HypnoMix Studio session (listed as findings; the new set is for Live only).
ducknew
Vocal reverb and delay that step back while the singer sings and bloom in the gaps: tunes what you have, or adds it. On a HypnoMix Studio session the compressors are written into the open engine only for the ids you pass with --engine --ids.
cliplevelnew
Finds single harmony clips sitting far under the rest of their track and offers a clip-gain raise.
unrollnew
A looped clip becomes one copy per pass, so a single pass can be nudged or changed on its own.
automation
List, show, offset or dip automation envelopes.
mine · audit · refs
Tempo, key, plug-in chains and routing; stacked limiters and bypassed devices; missing audio files. audit reads a Live set or a HypnoMix Studio session: in a session, ACE EQ's High Pass / Low Pass bands, ACE Compressor's Range, Auto Release and Mix, and Hypnomix Saturator's curve and drive show in each chain, and its EQ, limiter and doubled-compressor flags are the same as for a Live set (sends are not checked there).

Vocals & recordings

real instruments, real voices

prep
Raw takes → mix-ready stems: clicks, clipping, plosives, breaths, hum, hiss, silence, fades, gain staging. --plan turns every repair into an item you approve.
tune
Extremely gentle pitch correction to the song's own tuning: a 35% nudge, only notes over 20 cents off, never more than 25 cents, and only the moved notes are replaced. From a Live set it tunes the takes under your comp clips, so the comp stays yours. A take's pitch track is kept (in ~/.hypnomix/cache/pitch, named by the audio itself, so a changed take is always measured again), so the scan, the previews and the fix do not track the same take twice.
key
The song's key with a confidence, mapped section by section; names the one disputed note when it is unsure.
edgesnew
Finds the start of a word your comp loses: an “s” or “t” the take sings but a fade or a comp edit turns down, only where the word goes on right after. Fixes are clip edits on your own comp (a shorter fade, an earlier start, a later comp switch), re-checked until clean.
vocalign
Doubles and harmonies in time with each other, word by word, with presets, protected areas and sync points; optional pitch matching (chord-checked) and formant.
alignset · aligndevicenew
Aligns the vocal parts inside a Live set as warp markers on your own clips, in a new set; or from the Hypnomix Align device in Live. In REAPER, the same from the HypnoMix Align panel (hypnomix reaper align) or alignset Song.rpp: stretch markers or new takes on your own items, in a new project.
vocallevelnew
Does the vocal stay at an even level from lyric line to lyric line, and do the upper parts sit under the melody? Measured as heard. Run it in the first pass and again before every final export, because edits since then can change it. --apply writes the fixes as volume automation into a new set, or as volume envelope points into a new REAPER project (opened and read back on a two-track test project in REAPER 7.86 (2026-11-26), not on a real song and not judged by ear); approving one in the review writes nothing by itself. It never writes over a file: an existing --apply or --json name, the original under any spelling of its name, or a folder that does not exist is refused in one sentence. Envelope points far from every change are left exactly as they are, and a track whose envelope is, or would go, above +6 dB where it changes is refused and listed: make that change by hand.
voices
Balances harmonies around the part you choose, or splits them into one track per voice. --learn reads the spacing you use in your own songs; in a set it is judged as heard and written as volume automation.
dehiss · deess · breaths
Spectral de-hiss with a smart gate; a split-band de-esser; breaths turned down, not off. Each reports before it writes, and none writes over a file that already exists.
clipmatch
Evens out clips recorded at different gain or mic positions.
takes
Ranks takes by clipping, noise, pitch and timing; writes a comp list per section.
soundcheck
Listens to your input and tells you how to set the preamp. --watch is a live meter.
cleanup
Demo rescue: hum, noise, rumble, DC, silence.

breathenew

variationsnew
Unused takes of a part that fit the loop it keeps repeating, on a new switched-off track next to it (in HypnoMix Studio, --cues puts each A and B into clip slots to launch on the bar).
colournew
Where a part sounds the same every time round, proposes a new colour (width, grit, space, movement or an octave copy) as a chain of Live's own devices on a switched-off copy, each with a before and after clip. Proposals only. Live sets for now.

How automix decides, move by move

movethe rule behind it
RolesReads each stem's name first (kick, snr, bass, gtr, keys, vox, bv, pad…), then the sound. Name your stems: it's the single best thing you can do for the result.
Clean-up EQHigh-passes parts with nothing useful down low (vocals and guitars ~90 Hz, hats ~300 Hz); cuts resonances only when present in most of the song; wide cuts for mud (200–500 Hz) and harshness (2.5–5 kHz).
Tone EQOnly where needed: kick weight at 60 Hz and click at 4 kHz, bass harmonics around 800 Hz for small speakers, vocal presence at 3 kHz and air at 10 kHz.
CompressionSet by result, not threshold: vocal ~5 dB at 3:1, bass ~5 dB at 4:1, drum bus ~2 dB at 2:1. The vocal gets a split-band de-esser after it.
BalanceFrom each part's loudness while it is sounding: lead vocal on top, kick ~3 dB under, bass and snare ~4, guitars and keys ~7, pads ~12.
PanningKick, snare, bass and lead centred; doubles, backing vocals and keys spread in pairs; low end kept mono.
MaskingTwo parts fighting in the same band at the same time: the less important one gives way with a 2–3 dB cut. The lead vocal is never the one cut. Approve it and the cut is written into a new file as one band on that track's EQ, or on a new EQ added to its chain if it has none (EQ Eight in Live, ReaEQ in REAPER).
Space & glueOne shared plate (1.8 s, 25 ms pre-delay) puts everything in one room; kick and bass stay dry. Drums glued at 2:1, 30 ms attack, ~2 dB.

The mastering chain

stepwhat and whyin Live (stock)
1 · SubsonicHigh-pass at 22 Hz: energy no speaker plays still eats limiter headroom.EQ Eight
2 · Tone matchOptional: moves your tonal balance part of the way (default 60%, capped ±3 dB) toward a reference.EQ Eight
3 · Glue1.5:1, slow attack, ~1.5 dB. More than ~2 dB becomes an effect.Glue Compressor
4 · Mono lowsBelow 120 Hz, only if the low end is wide.Utility
5 · WarmthOptional saturation so bass is heard on small speakers.Saturator
6 · LimiterTrue-peak limiting to the loudness target, 4× oversampled.Limiter
7 · Codec-safeTest-encodes AAC 256 and MP3 320; lowers the ceiling if they'd clip.Limiter ceiling
8 · Top & tailHead silence trimmed to 50 ms, a 3 ms fade-in, a fade-out if the song ends abruptly.
That chain is set from a bounce. To master inside Live as you go, use HypnomixMain on Main: Build chain puts the same kind of chain in an editable rack of Live's own devices (Tone, Glue, Tame, Warmth, Width, Limiter, every gain at 0 dB), Match target turns the Limiter until Main sits on your loudness target, and Save keeps your edited chain, or a rack of your own, as a template.
