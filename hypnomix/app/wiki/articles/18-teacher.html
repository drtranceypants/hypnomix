HypnoMix is a free, open-source mixing and mastering assistant for Mac and Windows, in its own built-in DAW or as plug-ins inside Ableton Live and REAPER, and it is also a teacher. The Teacher tab teaches audio production for any kind of music, from what sound is to releasing a song, in the DAW you use, with audio examples, quizzes and a glossary. Learn the craft, then let HypnoMix take on the chores while you decide every change.

Three courses, one tab. Turn teacher mode on in the guided setup (the Your DAW step), or open the Teacher tab any time and pick a course. Each follows the DAW you chose, and the app remembers where you are.

Most lessons have common mistakes, an exercise to try in your own DAW and a quiz that marks itself, and many have Hear it audio examples (two or more play buttons, made on the spot on your computer: a low cut, compression with a slow and a fast attack, a limiter against clipping, and about twenty more). The Glossary button lists every word, A to Z, with a filter and a link to the lesson that teaches it.
The 101 crash coursepeople just getting into audio productionthe words and the screen of your DAW, then a whole song: a tempo, recording to a click, a drum beat to play over (optional), a track armed with the right input, setting the input gain, recording takes; balance and pan, then each effect as you add it (EQ, compression, de-essing, reverb and delay on sends, saturation, groups, automation), what it does, why, and where to start; then what mastering is and a good mastering chain. It ends with the tour. It is the shortest way through (37 steps in Live and in REAPER, 29 in HypnoMix Studio), and every step of it is also in the complete course.

Live, REAPER and HypnoMix Studio each get their own walkthrough. Ableton Live and REAPER get the same depth: every lesson says where to click in that DAW, and both can do the same hands-on steps. Live alone has a lesson on its Session View; REAPER alone has one on the HypnoMix plug-ins' built-in guide (the ? button in the top-right corner of HypnoMix Probe, HypnoMix Main, the Review panel and the Align panel). HypnoMix Studio gets the short version: HypnoMix reads and scans HypnoMix Studio sessions (.ardour) but in these lessons it does not drive the DAW, so every HypnoMix Studio step shows where to click. HypnoMix Studio (beta) is an add-on that can be added on Apple-silicon Macs (not available on Intel Macs or Windows yet, and nothing written for Studio has been judged by ear): see HypnoMix Studio.

Let HypnoMix do the hands-on steps. In Live (through the AbletonOSC connection) and REAPER (through its bridge) a step can do the job for you: set the tempo, switch the click on, add a four-bar drum beat on a MIDI track (Live: a kit from the Browser; REAPER: ReaSynDr), make an audio track on the right interface input, armed, record, add an effect with starting settings (EQ, compression, reverb, saturation), build the mastering chain, or make the devices. Each one first says exactly what it will change and waits for Do it; anything it couldn't set is listed for you to set by hand, and your DAW's undo takes it back. Check my level listens to your interface for eight seconds and says which way to turn the gain; it records nothing.

No AI needed. Every lesson is in the app. With the Assistant set up, Ask the Assistant opens a chat about the step you're on (nothing is sent until you press Send), and while teacher mode is on the Assistant teaches at your course's level.

The Wiki follows your DAW. Its pages for the DAW you chose come first, after Start here, and the other DAWs' pages move to the end. The courses are in the Wiki too, under each DAW: "The complete course: make a song from scratch in …" is the contents page and links to one article per chapter ("Sound and hearing", "Mixing", …), and "Guided tour: HypnoMix in …" is the tour. One Glossary article lists every word. The audio examples play in the app's Teacher tab only.

Tested in Live 12.4 and REAPER 7.86. In Live, an audio track can only be armed once Live has an audio input: if Settings → Audio has no Audio Input Device, the track step says so. In REAPER the beat plays on ReaSynDr, REAPER's own simple drum synth (its voices sit on notes 32 to 35); load any drum plug-in on the track for a real kit. On Live's return A, the reverb step sets the Reverb that's already there instead of adding a second one. In REAPER it makes a track called Reverb with ReaVerbate (or sets the one already there); REAPER's bridge can't make sends, so you send your tracks to it yourself, and the step says how. Both were run in REAPER 7.86 (2026-11-26, on a test project): ReaVerbate reads back Wet 0 dB with Dry off, and JS: Saturation reads back Amount 20%. The REAPER A to Z menu paths, render options and stock plug-in control names were verified against REAPER 7.86 on macOS (2026-11-27); nothing was tried on Windows.

Setup remembers your choice. The guided setup's Your DAW step offers "No thanks", the tour, the 101 crash course and the complete course; you can switch in the Teacher tab at any time, and your place in each is kept (steps keep the same names across courses). The audio examples are made when you press play, from sounds the app synthesises, so nothing is downloaded; they are cached for two weeks in ~/.hypnomix/cache/teach and the disk tidy clears them.

Live drum beats and new tracks need this version's AbletonOSC script: Setup → Ableton Live offers to update it (the old copy is kept as a backup), then restart Live.
