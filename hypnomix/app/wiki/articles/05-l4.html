• Amount. Approve a fix at 60% instead of all or nothing. It works for tuning, fades, clip gain, level and volume moves, device settings, timing nudges and frequency-fight cuts. Fixes that can't be scaled say so. Timing and tuning findings start at the amount you saved in Preferences (100% until you save one), and no amount goes past the tool's own gentle limit. Re-make the preview at this amount builds the before and after clip again at the slider's value.
  • The key. A tuning finding shows the key the scan detected and how sure it was. Choose another note and major or minor, or confirm the detected one, with Use this key: the song's tuning is proposed again in that key and the key is saved for the song. The chord under every note is still checked before it moves.
  • Apply to the ones like this. Decide one small click fix and apply the same decision to the 14 like it, after seeing the list. Anything you decided one by one stays as you decided it.
  • Why. With teaching on (the default), each finding explains the technique in two or three sentences: why a fade stops a click, why a slower release keeps the bass round.
  • A question budget. Tell it how many questions you want per song. The rest go into a "low priority" batch with a suggested decision for each group.
$ hypnomix fix "hypnomix-out/scan/Song" --decide tune-Lead=approve@60
$ hypnomix fix "hypnomix-out/scan/Song" --decide-like smooth-3=approve --dry-run   # see what it matches first
$ hypnomix prefs set review.max_questions=12 review.teach=on
$ hypnomix prefs set taste.timing_amount=50 taste.tuning_amount=60   # where those sliders start
$ hypnomix scan --retune "hypnomix-out/scan/Song" --key "A minor"   # the tuning again, in the key you choose
