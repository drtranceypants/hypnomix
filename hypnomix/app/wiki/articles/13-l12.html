optionwhat it does
tonal build · tonal checka target tone range from your releases or songs you choose; where your mix sits outside it
--amount 0-100scales every mastering move: 100 is the full proposal, 50 is half of each
--stabilize TARGETslow dynamic EQ that moves a band back toward the target only when it drifts out
--dyneqyour own dynamic EQ bands: frequency, threshold, ratio, width
--width-bands · --mono-belowstereo width per frequency range; a mono low end
--limiter-mode clean|punch|loudthe limiter's character; every run reports loudness gained against punch lost
--harsh · harshfinds and gently tames build-ups in the 2–6 kHz range only where they happen
--ditherproper dither on 16- and 24-bit files
rebalanceturn the vocal, bass or drums up or down inside a finished stereo mix
translate --previewhear it as MP3 and AAC, and as a streaming service plays it
$ hypnomix tonal build "Release 1.wav" "Release 2.wav" "Release 3.wav" --name mine
$ hypnomix tonal check "Song mix.wav" --target mine
$ hypnomix master "Song mix.wav" --target -11 --limiter-mode punch --stabilize mine --amount 80 --plan
$ hypnomix rebalance "Song master.wav" --vocals +1.5
$ hypnomix translate "Song master.wav" --preview
Mastering inside Live: HypnomixMain can build this kind of chain from Live's own devices on Main, follow your song card's ceiling and character, and match the loudness target while the song plays. See lesson 7.
