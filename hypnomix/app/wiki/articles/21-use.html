1 · Mix a folder of stems, start to finish

$ hypnomix automix "Night Drive stems/" --name "Night Drive" --reference "reference.wav"
$ hypnomix translate "hypnomix-out/automix/Night Drive/Night Drive (master).wav" --render
$ hypnomix qc "hypnomix-out/automix/Night Drive/Night Drive (master).wav"

Read MIX NOTES.md, listen on headphones and a phone, and change what your ears disagree with: usually vocal level and reverb amount.

2 · Master a finished mix

$ hypnomix master "Song mix.wav" --target -14
$ hypnomix master "Song mix.wav" --target -14 --reference "reference.wav" --match 0.6
$ hypnomix chain master "Song bounce.wav" --target -14 --verify --existing bypass   # same, built in Live

3 · Clean up raw recordings

$ hypnomix prep "raw stems/"
$ hypnomix dehiss "Lead Vox.wav" --reduce 12 --gate 18 --report
$ hypnomix vocalign "Lead.wav" "Double.wav" "Harmony hi.wav" --trim-tails --level -6

4 · First pass on a song: scan → hear → review → fix

$ hypnomix prefs                                                    # once: targets, protected tracks
$ hypnomix preflight "raw takes/"                                    # first: are the files in good shape? (changes nothing)
$ hypnomix scan "Song.als" --mix "Song mix.wav" --key "F# major"   # read-only → REVIEW.md
$ hypnomix scan "Song.rpp"                                          # or a REAPER project
$ hypnomix scan "Song/"                                             # or a HypnoMix DAW session folder
$ hypnomix scan "raw takes/"                                        # or a folder from any DAW
$ hypnomix ab "hypnomix-out/scan/Song"                              # before/after clips, level-matched (clips, tuning, timing, device changes it can model)
$ hypnomix review "hypnomix-out/scan/Song" --serve                  # a page: play A, play B, click
$ hypnomix fix "hypnomix-out/scan/Song" --approved --out "Song (fixed).als"
$ hypnomix handoff "hypnomix-out/scan/Song"                        # what is left to do by hand, in your DAW's words, with markers

Every check runs at once, nothing changes until you approve it, and approved changes land in one new set next to the original, which fix then checks and measures again to confirm each problem is gone. Close the set in Live before fix. No song of your own to try it on? hypnomix demo writes a 40-second song with six labelled problems.

5 · Hands on your open set

$ hypnomix live snapshot                                   # always first
$ hypnomix live db "Lead Vox" -3.5
$ hypnomix live paramd "Lead Vox" "EQ Eight" "1 Frequency A" 2500
$ hypnomix loop --bar 49 --bars 8 --ab "db Bass -1.5" --revert   # the A/B habit

6 · Recording a part

$ hypnomix soundcheck --watch --device "USB Audio"     # set the preamp: peaks -12 to -6 dBFS
$ hypnomix live input "Gtr" "Ext. In" "2" && hypnomix live arm "Gtr" 1
$ hypnomix live record 17 8 3                          # bars 17–24, three takes
$ hypnomix takes "Gtr take 1.wav" "Gtr take 2.wav" "Gtr take 3.wav" --bpm 92

7 · A harmony stack that sits right

$ hypnomix voices --learn "Finished song 1.als" "Finished song 2.als"   # your own spacing, once
$ hypnomix voices "Lead.wav" "Harmony lo.wav" "Harmony hi.wav"           # lists the parts and asks
$ hypnomix voices "Lead.wav" "Harmony lo.wav" "Harmony hi.wav" --anchor lead --tame
$ hypnomix tune "Lead.wav" --ref "Song mix.wav" --key "F# major" --report-only

8 · A drum take played without a click

$ hypnomix tempomap "Drum take.wav" --click "check.wav"       # listen: is the click on the beat?
$ hypnomix tempomap "Jam.als" --track "Drums" --conform --preview "conformed.wav" --apply "Jam (conformed).als"
$ hypnomix tempomap "Jam.als" --track "Drums" --follow --apply "Jam (tempo map).als"   # or: the grid follows the drummer
$ hypnomix loops "Jam.als" --track "Drums" --apply "Jam (loops).als"                  # best loops into Session slots

9 · Tighter, but still played by a person

$ hypnomix timing "Song.als" --track Drums --track Bass --track Guitar    # only the hits out of time with the band
$ hypnomix tighten "Song.als" --track "Drums" --strength 0.5 --preview "tighten preview/"
$ hypnomix breathe "Song.als" --mix "Song mix.wav" --preview "Song (breathing).wav"    # choruses push, verses relax

10 · A song that loops too much

$ hypnomix variations "Song.als"                              # unused takes that fit; A/B files to audition
$ hypnomix variations "Song.als" --apply "Song (variations).als"   # new tracks, switched off
$ hypnomix borrow "Song.als" --mix "Song mix.wav" --plan    # loops the original project had
$ hypnomix motion --learn "Finished song.als"                # your own effect moves, once
$ hypnomix motion "Song.als" --mix "Song mix.wav" --plan    # risers, dips, throws: options to approve

Everything in recipes 8 to 10 arrives as an option: a preview file, a switched-off track, or an item in the review. Turn it on, listen in the mix, keep or delete.

11 · Package the release

$ hypnomix release "Song (master).wav" --title "Song" --artist "Me" --art cover.jpg --check   # read-only: what would be made, and what fails
$ hypnomix release "Song (master).wav" --title "Song" --artist "Me" --art cover.jpg           # a new folder: WAV 24-bit and 16-bit, MP3, AAC
$ hypnomix versions --stems "Song stems/" --vocals "Lead*,Harm*" --mix "Song (master).wav" --check   # instrumental, a cappella, TV mix, radio edit
$ hypnomix album "Song 1.wav" "Song 2.wav" "Song 3.wav"                                          # do the songs sit together? (report first)

12 · Is the new bounce better than the last one?

$ hypnomix compare "Song (bounce 1).wav" "Song (bounce 2).wav"       # loudness, tone, width, each section; A/B clips of the biggest differences
$ hypnomix vocallevel "Song.als" --heard "solo exports/"            # before every final export: is the vocal level even, line to line?
