hypnomix is a free, open-source mixing and mastering engineer that lives on your Mac or Windows PC and works with Ableton Live, REAPER and Ardour. It was built by Dr. Trance, who writes, plays, records, mixes and masters every part of his own music, for people who record real instruments and vocals, not just MIDI.

You can use it three ways. First pass: point it at a Live set, a REAPER project, an Ardour session, or a folder of recordings from any DAW. It runs every check at once, changes nothing, and writes one review list with before-and-after clips. You approve, skip or change each item, and only the approved fixes go into a new set next to your original. Engineer mode: give it a folder of stems and it cleans them up, balances them, compresses, EQs, pans, adds reverb and masters the result, then writes MIX NOTES.md explaining every decision. Hands-on mode: keep your own session and let hypnomix set exact fader levels and plug-in parameters in the open Live set, record A/B loops, and measure what each change did. In REAPER it builds the mastering chain in the open session.

How it decides. Everything is measurement plus the rules an engineer would use. There are no black-box models in the mixing chain, so every decision can be written down and argued with. Its checks measure rather than listen; for an opinion by ear, the optional Listener lets a Google model hear a bounce. Treat its work as a strong first pass by an assistant, then make the taste calls yourself.

It learns how you work. It asks your preferences once (loudness target, tracks never to touch, what is deliberate), reads your own finished songs to learn your habits (how far your harmonies sit under the lead, how you pan, which effect moves you draw), and remembers what you approve and skip. Proposals then start from your standards, not from someone else's numbers.

It keeps the performance human. Timing fixes move only the hits that are clearly out, and only part of the way. Tuning moves the centre of a note and keeps the vibrato and slides. A full snap to the grid is refused.

It never needs a paid plug-in. Its own processing is built in, and anything it does offline it can also build inside Live from the stock devices every Live Standard or Suite owner already has, or in REAPER from its own stock plug-ins.

New to audio? The app's Teacher tab is a free course in your own DAW: the complete course from what sound is to releasing a song, a 101 crash course, or a tour of hypnomix for engineers.
