hypnomix works the same way everywhere: it scans, you approve, it writes only what you approved into a new project or new files. What changes is which DAW you use and how you drive it. Pick one of each.

Which download

The download page offers the Assistant only today. The app's guided setup describes both, so you can note that you want the full one for when it is out:

downloadforwhat's in itwhen
Hypnomix Assistantpeople who keep their own DAW: Live, REAPER, Ardour, or any DAW from exported tracksthe app and every hypnomix tool (the installers for Mac and Windows)now
Hypnomixpeople who want one program: a DAW with the engineer and the Assistant built ina DAW of its own (based on Ardour) in the same app: it opens on the DAW tab, with File → New Session and Open Session, plus everything the Assistant hasnot available yet, and Mac only at first. It will be a separate download that replaces the Assistant app (the same Hypnomix.app), not something the Assistant adds to itself: nothing in Setup → Extras installs it, that step only explains what it is. The engine inside it is a separate program under the GNU General Public License (version 2 or later), a rebranded fork of Ardour; hypnomix itself stays MIT and contains none of its code. The source will be published with the first release that includes the engine. Not yet checked: playing through a real sound card (only the silent test backend has been run), recording, the app's native File menu, the full app's disk image, signing and notarising it, and Intel Macs.

New to hypnomix, or to audio? Choose your DAW in the app's guided setup and turn on teacher mode: a tour of hypnomix and its devices in your DAW, a 101 crash course from an empty song to a master, or the complete course from what sound is to releasing a song. The app's Wiki then shows your DAW's pages first.

Any way works with any DAW above. You can also mix them: click through the review in the app, then ask an agent app for the master.

What has been checked where. The Live devices and the Live fix paths were tested in Live 12, and REAPER support in REAPER 7.86. The newest additions (the new commands, EQ bands written for masking cuts, ab previews of timing nudges and device changes, and fix on an Ardour scan) pass unit tests and hypnomix's own structure checks on synthetic data. The REAPER files they write (the demo project, a project written by fix, ReaEQ masking bands, the hand-off marker and the A/B project) were opened in REAPER 7.86 and read back as written. The new Live sets and the Ardour snapshots have not been opened in their DAWs yet, the new tools have not been run on real songs, and nothing new has been judged by ear. What comes next.
